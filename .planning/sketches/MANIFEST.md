# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction
Pure arcade block puzzle game with a premium, tactile, adult-focused aesthetic. 
- **Non-cluttered & Simple:** Avoid overly busy 3D meshes or messy fantasy illustrations. Focus on elegant geometric silhouettes that look instantly touchable.
- **Restrained Color Grading:** Rich, deep jewel tones (Sapphire, Emerald, Amber, Amethyst) with refined metallic and glass-like edge highlights rather than loud or cheap neon saturation.
- **Clickable Polish:** High-contrast icons that immediately stand out on a phone's app launcher.

## Reference Points
- Alto's Adventure & Monument Valley (restrained, tasteful geometry, high elegance)
- Lumines & Threes! (clean arcade typography, tactile physical tiles)
- Premium Apple Design Award mobile icons (tangible material depth without fake skeuomorphic noise)
