# Sketch 001: App Icon & Brand Visuals

## Design Question
What visual silhouette and material treatment gives Block Battles an instantly magnetic, premium, tactile presence on a mobile home screen without relying on cluttered 3D meshes or loud neon saturation?

## The 3 Explored Variants

### 1. Variant A: "The Monolith Gem" (Minimalist Tactile Silhouette)
- **Concept:** A single, mathematically centered sapphire tile embedded into an obsidian slate squircle with a hairline titanium rim.
- **Center Piece:** Deep royal sapphire with a subtle warm gold pip at its core.
- **Vibe:** Pure zen, architectural, like an Apple Design Award luxury puzzle tile.

### 2. Variant B: "The Spatial Dual" (Interlocking Puzzle Geometry)
- **Concept:** Two interlocking 2D puzzle shapes (an Emerald Green L-tromino and an Amethyst Purple block) separated by a clean 2px spatial groove.
- **Vibe:** Immediate gameplay legibility. A user glancing at their screen instantly understands it is a spatial block-fitting puzzle.

### 3. Variant C: "The Golden Quadrant" (2×2 Grid with Key Piece)
- **Concept:** A 2×2 grid where three muted slate blocks form the backdrop, and one elevated warm liquid-gold tile radiates tactile weight.
- **Vibe:** High contrast, luxury arcade token. Maximum click-through incentive.

## Interactive Features Included in Sketch
1. **Interactive Size Switcher:** Test icons at `512px` (Master store asset), `192px` (Android launcher), and `72px` (Real phone grid density).
2. **Mobile Home Screen Simulator:** Preview how each icon sits in a realistic phone launcher grid next to common apps (Chrome, Spotify, Camera, Photos) on different wallpapers (OLED dark, Deep Indigo, Titanium Slate).
3. **Tactile Touch Feedback:** Tap or click any icon to test the physical squash-and-spring touch feedback.
