**Inheritance confirmation:** This document respects the solo-developer scope of Doc 00 and the UI cleanliness mandated in Doc 08. It introduces a commercial layer that sustains the product without violating the "Confident Croupier" tone (Doc 15) or the flow state of the puzzle mechanics.

## 1. Monetization Philosophy

**"Revenue through Retention, not Extraction."**

Block Battles relies on the tactile satisfaction of its core loop (`12_ANIMATION_MOTION_AND_GAME_FEEL.md`). If the monetization disrupts the flow state, players will leave before generating meaningful Lifetime Value (LTV). 

The monetization strategy must be:
1. **Transparent:** The player always knows exactly what they are paying for, or what they get for watching an ad.
2. **Opt-in Heavy:** Value player agency. Forced interruptions are absolute last resorts.
3. **Gameplay-First:** Monetization cannot alter the RNG (`04_SPAWN_RNG_AND_DIFFICULTY.md`) to force failures and sell revives. The game must remain mathematically fair.
4. **Clean:** No pulsing "SALE" badges, no UI clutter, no disguised buttons.

---

## 2. MVP Monetization Strategy

The smallest, most ethical viable approach for a solo developer.

### 2.1 The MVP Strategy
1. **Single IAP (In-App Purchase):** "Premium Unlock" (Removes all forced ads, grants a permanent cosmetic).
2. **Rewarded Ads (Opt-In):** Used for post-run reward multipliers and one-time revives.
3. **Interstitials (Forced):** Only between runs (never mid-run), and only for non-Premium players.

### 2.2 What is EXPLICITLY REJECTED for MVP
- **Energy Systems / Lives:** Artificial session limits punish retention.
- **Multiple Currencies:** (e.g., Gold + Premium Gems). Confusing and predatory.
- **Loot Boxes / Gacha:** Misaligned with a skill-based puzzle game.
- **Battle Pass:** Requires a massive content treadmill that a solo dev cannot sustain.
- **Pay-to-Win Powerups:** Buying "Undo" or "Clear Board" boosters destroys the puzzle integrity.
- **Subscriptions:** Not justified by the game's service model.

---

## 3. Rewarded Ad System

Rewarded video ads are the most acceptable form of mobile monetization because they respect player agency.

| Property | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Trigger Location** | Post-Run Results Screen. |
| **User Choice** | Explicit button: "Watch Ad to Double Stardust". Never disguised. |
| **Reward** | 2x multiplier on the metaprogression currency earned during that run. |
| **Frequency** | Once per run completion. |
| **Limits** | No daily cap on post-run multiplier (if they play 10 runs, they can watch 10 ads). |
| **Cooldown** | N/A (Run length naturally spaces them out). |

---

## 4. Revive UX

Block Battles does not have Player HP. Players lose via **Board Lock** or **Move Limit**. A revive ("Second Wind") must temporarily alter the board to allow continuation.

| Property | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Game-Over State** | Triggered immediately upon failure state detection. |
| **Revive Offer** | Modal: "Board Locked. Watch Ad for a Second Wind?" |
| **Reward (Board Lock)** | Clears the bottom 3 rows of the board, allowing new placements. |
| **Reward (Move Limit)** | Adds 10 Moves to the counter. |
| **Cost** | 1 Rewarded Ad view. (Premium players get 1 free revive per run without the ad). |
| **Eligibility** | Allowed exactly **once per run**. No chaining revives to brute-force a win. |
| **Decline Behavior** | Tapping "End Run" immediately proceeds to the Run Results screen. No "Are you sure?" guilt-trip prompts. |

---

## 5. Interstitial Ads

Interstitials (forced ads) are highly detrimental to the flow state. They are included only because mobile F2P economics often demand a baseline revenue floor from non-converting players.

### 5.1 Strict Rules for Interstitials
- **Trigger Timing:** ONLY triggers on the transition from the Run Results screen back to the Home Screen. 
- **Prohibited Moments:** NEVER mid-battle. NEVER between battles within the same run. NEVER on app launch.
- **Frequency Cap:** Maximum 1 interstitial per 5 minutes of gameplay. If a run lasts 2 minutes and the player dies, they do not see an interstitial.
- **Premium Exemption:** Completely disabled if the player purchases the "Premium Unlock" IAP.

---

## 6. Banner Ads

**Banner Ads are explicitly PROHIBITED in Block Battles.**

*Rationale:* Banners destroy the carefully crafted aspect ratio layout (`09` Section 17), introduce visual noise that competes with the board, and cause accidental clicks that infuriate players. The revenue generated by banners does not justify the immense damage to the premium UI aesthetic.

---

## 7. Reward Economy

The MVP economy is restricted to a single "Soft" currency to keep the system extremely small.

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Currency Name** | Stardust |
| **Earning Method** | Awarded at the end of a run. Scales with Score, Bosses Defeated, and Run Depth. |
| **Persistence** | Permanent. Survives between runs. |
| **Primary Sink** | Unlocking new Relics in the Compendium. |
| **Secondary Sink** | (MVP+) Unlocking new visual themes. |

There is no "Hard" (premium) currency. Stardust cannot be bought with real money. It can only be earned by playing.

---

## 8. Persistent Unlocks

To drive long-term retention without breaking game balance, unlocks provide **horizontal variety, not vertical power**.

- **What unlocks:** New Relics.
- **Mechanism:** The player spends Stardust in the Home Screen Compendium to unlock a Relic.
- **Effect:** The unlocked Relic is added to the randomized drop pool for future runs. It does not equip immediately. 
- **Fairness:** An unlocked relic is a new strategic option, not an automatic win button.

---

## 9. Cosmetic Monetization (MVP+)

If cosmetics are added later, they must adhere to these rules:

- **What changes:** Board surface tint, Background color/gradient, Block material theme (e.g., "Neon Acrylic" vs "Classic Wood").
- **What NEVER changes:** Piece shapes, UI layout, Accessibility readability, Hitbox sizes.
- **Gameplay Fairness:** A cosmetic skin cannot grant a gameplay advantage (e.g., no skin can make valid ghost placement lines thicker or more obvious than the default).

---

## 10. Shop UX

The Shop is a single, clean modal accessible from the Home screen.

**Contents at MVP:**
1. **Premium Unlock (Remove Ads)** — Real Money.
2. **Support the Dev (Tip Jar)** — Optional Real Money tiers (e.g., $1, $3, $5) that grant a small, non-exclusive cosmetic badge on the Home Screen.

**UX Rules:** No scrolling grids of bundled currencies. No confusing math ("Best Value! 300% Extra!"). It is a simple storefront.

---

## 11. Purchase Safety

- **Confirmation:** All real-money purchases use the native OS purchase flow (Apple App Store / Google Play Billing).
- **Duplicate Prevention:** The "Premium Unlock" button must disable and change to "Purchased" upon receipt verification.
- **Restore Purchases:** A prominent "Restore Purchases" button must exist in the Shop and Settings menus (required by Apple/Google guidelines).
- **Transparency:** The exact price in local currency must be visible *before* the user taps the button.

---

## 12. Player Trust Rules

These are binding ethical UX constraints.

1. **No Misleading Buttons:** The "Decline Ad" button must be visually clear, at least 14sp text, and must appear at the exact same time as the "Watch Ad" button. No delayed appearance for the decline button.
2. **No Fake Urgency:** No countdown timers forcing a rapid decision on revives or purchases.
3. **No Disguised Ads:** A button that triggers an ad must explicitly state "Watch Ad" or contain standard video ad iconography.
4. **No Guilt Tripping:** Do not use copy like "No thanks, I want to lose my progress." Use "End Run."
5. **No False Scarcity:** Do not label a permanent shop item as "Limited Time Only!"

---

## 13. Monetization Placement Matrix

| Monetization Event | Trigger | Frequency | Player Choice | Gameplay Impact | UX Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Second Wind (Revive)** | Run Failure | 1x per Run | High (Opt-in) | Alters board/moves | Low |
| **2x Stardust** | Run Results | 1x per Run | High (Opt-in) | Economy boost | Low |
| **Interstitial** | Return to Menu | 1x per 5 mins | None (Forced) | None | High (Flow break) |
| **Premium Unlock**| Menu Shop | 1x Lifetime | High (Opt-in) | Removes interstitials | None |

---

## 14. Revenue vs Retention Analysis

**The Trade-off:** Increasing interstitial frequency directly increases short-term Ad ARPDAU (Average Revenue Per Daily Active User), but exponentially increases Day-1 and Day-7 churn.

**The Strategy:** Block Battles targets a niche of players seeking a premium, flow-state puzzle experience. Aggressive ad spam will cause this specific demographic to churn immediately. By limiting forced ads to post-run and leaning heavily on high-value Rewarded Ads (which players actually *like* using for multipliers/revives), the game maximizes long-term retention, leading to higher LTV and better app store ratings.

---

## 15. A/B Testing Considerations (MVP+)

If monetization tuning is necessary post-launch, these variables are "safe" to A/B test without breaking player trust:
- Interstitial cooldown timer (e.g., 5 mins vs 8 mins).
- The Stardust multiplier value on the rewarded ad (e.g., 1.5x vs 2.0x).
- The price point of the Premium Unlock (e.g., $2.99 vs $4.99).

**Unsafe Testing:** NEVER A/B test core puzzle mechanics (RNG drop rates, Move limits) to artificially increase failure rates for a test cohort just to drive revive ad impressions.

---

## 16. Failure Modes

| Failure Mode | Symptom | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Ad Fatigue** | Ad fill rates drop; players ignore rewarded ads. | Ensure the reward (Stardust) always feels valuable. Ensure the ad frequency cap prevents spam. |
| **Reward Inflation**| Players unlock all Relics too fast, making Stardust useless. | Ensure Stardust costs scale exponentially, or introduce visual themes as an endgame Stardust sink. |
| **Revive Dependency**| Game feels impossible to win without watching the Revive ad. | Ensure baseline difficulty is tuned so that skilled players win without revives. The revive is a safety net, not a requirement. |
| **P2W Perception** | Players believe the game forces losses to sell the Premium Unlock. | Keep the Premium Unlock strictly focused on Ads and Cosmetics, never gameplay power. |

## 17. Analytics Requirements

Only collect telemetry necessary to monitor the health of the game economy and ad network.

- `ad_impression_interstitial`
- `ad_impression_rewarded`
- `ad_reward_claimed` (tagged by type: revive vs multiplier)
- `iap_purchase_completed` (tagged by item ID)
- `shop_screen_opened`
- `run_abandoned_post_ad` (measures if players quit out of frustration after an ad)

---

## 18. Accessibility & Privacy Considerations

- **Privacy Compliance:** Integration of any Ad SDK (AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin) must include COPPA compliance for minors and an explicit Consent Management Platform (CMP) popup for GDPR/CCPA.
- **iOS ATT:** The App Tracking Transparency prompt must be contextualized. (e.g., "We use ads to keep the game free. Allowing tracking provides more relevant ads.")
- **Audio Accessibility:** Ad SDKs must be configured to respect the hardware mute switch so video ads do not blast audio in quiet environments.

---

## 19. Monetization Acceptance Criteria

No commercial feature merges to the main branch unless:
- [ ] The "Premium Unlock" successfully disables all interstitial logic immediately upon purchase.
- [ ] A player can complete a full 12-battle run without seeing a single forced ad.
- [ ] The Revive ad clears the board/adds moves exactly as stated, and never triggers a second time in the same run.
- [ ] The "Decline" button for any rewarded ad is instantly visible and easily tappable.
- [ ] The Shop UI scales correctly on small phones without hiding the Restore Purchases button.

---

## 20. Final Monetization Contract

> **Block Battles is a game first, and a product second.**
>
> We will earn our revenue by providing a premium, satisfying experience that players want to return to, not by trapping them in a skinner box. 
>
> We offer players a clear choice: support the game with your time (watching highly-rewarded, opt-in ads) or support the game with your wallet (a single, fair-priced Premium Unlock). We will not double-dip, we will not trick them, and we will never sacrifice the integrity of the puzzle to make a quick dollar.
